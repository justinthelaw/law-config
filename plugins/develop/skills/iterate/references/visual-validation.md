# Visual validation

Use for affected rendered surfaces within the selected implementation or review scope. Reuse existing authorized browser, native-app or artifact tools; this guidance does not authorize installation, access changes or bypassing host controls.

1. Identify the selected surface, state and observable acceptance criteria. Before changing the surface or exercising the target action, inspect its actual DOM or available native structure together with a current screenshot. Source code, style rules and inferred layout alone do not establish what the user sees. For a reported defect, reproduce the selected issue and retain a baseline; if it cannot be reproduced, record the conditions and uncertainty before claiming a fix.
2. Make the smallest supported change and preserve unrelated views, behavior and host controls. Exercise the relevant interaction after the change and inspect fresh structure and screenshots for the resulting states. Record what was directly observed separately from behavior inferred from source or tests.
3. Check applicable themes and wide, narrow and short viewports, choosing dimensions from the affected surface and its supported constraints. Inspect wrapping, clipping, overlap, alignment, readable content and reachable controls. Verify containment within the intended panel and internal scrolling where required; scrolling or expansion must not obscure controls or displace unrelated content. Record the dimensions, theme and state actually inspected rather than implying every combination passed.
4. Match evidence to its surface. A browser rendering or screenshot can establish browser behavior; it cannot certify a native host surface, embedded host controls or host-specific interaction. Inspect the actual native surface with permitted tools when that behavior is in scope. If tools, access or startup are unavailable, report the specific unverified surface or state and continue checks that remain possible.

Keep concise before/after evidence and any remaining gaps in the [validation handoff](validation.md). Screenshots support visual observations; they do not replace interaction, accessibility or other required behavioral checks. Do not alter unrelated surfaces or weaken controls to manufacture a successful capture.
