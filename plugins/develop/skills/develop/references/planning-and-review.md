# Planning and review

## Applicable planning

Plan in proportion to the task. Record consequential decisions and assumptions when useful; reuse valid decisions at handoff. Ask when a missing decision materially changes the work or authority. A routine implementation request does not require a separate plan approval. For consequential design, consider affected owners and callers, alternatives, compatibility and failure risks, and concrete acceptance checks. For interface work, consider the user flow, relevant states, accessibility, responsiveness and rendered behavior; reuse existing components.

## Review gates

Use independent review when requested, required by the repository or host, or warranted by an identified risk. Choose scope and budget to fit the task. Review current artifacts and evidence, resolve material findings within authority, and report missing coverage. Give independent reviewers the relevant intent, current artifact, criteria, evidence and a bounded question. Verify their findings and report coverage and limitations. A review never grants permission or replaces required checks or human approval.
