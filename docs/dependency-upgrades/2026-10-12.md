## Constraints and retry conditions

Native transitive graphs belong to their pinned hook/package parents. Newer leaves are not independent repository pins. Revisit each deferred leaf when its parent publishes a compatible release; regenerate the entire native graph rather than editing installed packages. Actionlint retains its upstream `go.mod`, including yaml/v4's release candidate: no additional prerelease was introduced. Host-managed rows retain official installation policies because no target-host inventory is available.
