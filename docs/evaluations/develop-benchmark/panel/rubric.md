# PR proposal rubric v2

The unit is one repository at one pinned pre-merge tree. Each generated submission supplies a patch and its final PR title and body. The third submission uses the merged diff and the upstream title and body observed at collection. Apply the same code and prose criteria to all three. Score absent historical text as unavailable.

| Gate | Pass condition | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligibility | A nonempty patch applies to the pinned base and the worker's terminal answer contains a title, blank line and nonempty body by the equal arm deadline. The upstream diff replays to its exact merged tree. | Release, sealed patch and proposal, apply log and monotonic clock. Timing applies to the two workers only. |
| Build and existing behavior | Selected offline build and native regressions pass. Immutable controls exercise previously working behavior. | Pinned toolchain, commands, base and submission output. Document the slice of the suite covered. |
| Requested behavior | A hidden public-interface check distinguishes starting and repaired behavior. Additional contract or differential checks can fail on any submission, including the merge. | Require a reached behavioral mismatch on base. A compiler, dependency or setup failure cannot qualify as a distinguishing result. |
| Integrity and safety | Verified bypass of validation, materially weakened applicable protection, or a demonstrated critical regression vetoes readiness. | Cite the changed path and a runnable counterexample or mechanical evidence. A judge suspicion goes to adjudication. Preserve an unresolved flag as pending. |

The fail-to-pass and pass-to-pass split follows SWE-bench Verified. Its follow-up audit found tests that required an unstated implementation. This panel tests stated public behavior and records specification-derived exceptions to the merged reference. [SWE-bench Verified](https://openai.com/index/introducing-swe-bench-verified/) · [SWE-bench audit](https://openai.com/index/why-we-no-longer-evaluate-swe-bench-verified/)

| Scored dimension | Weight | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Contract and compatibility | 35 | Misses the primary contract or introduces a serious regression | Main path works, with a material missing edge or unclear compatibility | All stated paths have affirmative evidence, including relevant boundaries and existing consumers |
| Regression protection | 25 | No meaningful protection, or tests would pass with the bug | Tests distinguish the main defect, but miss a material path | Focused, discriminating regression coverage includes the important distinct paths and keeps existing checks meaningful |
| Design and scope | 25 | Unsafe or disproportionate change that a maintainer would have to replace | Viable approach with specific avoidable complexity or unrelated edits | Fits repository APIs and idioms, addresses root cause, and keeps the diff as small as the behavior permits |
| PR communication | 15 | Missing, misleading, or materially overclaims verification | Describes the change but leaves meaningful motivation, verification, or risk unclear | Gives a precise title, explains what and why, reports checks accurately and identifies material limitations |

Use 1 or 3 for evidence between neighboring anchors. An unobserved property is unknown. Use the score for the observed submission, and mark unknown when the missing evidence prevents assigning an anchor. A brief, accurate PR can earn full communication credit. Extra words, change size, stylistic similarity to the upstream diff, and a claimed test pass carry no automatic credit. Record executable outcomes independently of the 0–100 score. A hard failure overrides the score for readiness. These weights and thresholds are panel choices. [Google code review criteria](https://google.github.io/eng-practices/review/reviewer/looking-for.html) · [Google change descriptions](https://google.github.io/eng-practices/review/developer/cl-descriptions.html)

Three fresh plugin-free judges see the same starting code, proposal text, patches and verified stage summaries. The collector masks explicit workflow and author disclosures, assigns opaque labels and rotates each submission through all three positions in the initial prompt. Larger proposals or patches use equal excerpt limits and link to their full local files. Judges can inspect files in any order after that initial display. Save raw inputs, mapping, model, judgments, cited evidence, order and evaluator spend. Keep root traces, timing, cost, historical PR URLs and the private key outside the packet. Flag recognizable provenance. Compute a per-axis median with at least two observations. Report score ranges, missing axes and preference reversals. A final soft preference requires three valid rotations, at least two agreeing on direction and a median paired weighted difference of at least five points. Partial scores remain provisional. A human maintainer can supply a separately identified and signed-off adjudication. Position balancing does not remove shared-model bias. [Zheng et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685)

Publish each repository and denominator. Within separately reported complete pairs, show ordinary and Develop costs, token classes, prompt-dispatch wall and checkout-to-proposal wall. Also report setup, grading, evaluator costs, interrupted runs and total elapsed campaign time. Use the deployment's observed currencies only when billing receipts exist. The Standard Codex estimate for GPT-6.1 Sol is USD 2 per million uncached inputs, 0.10 per million cached inputs and 10 per million outputs, including reasoning. Human open-to-merge time includes queues and review. Human labor and cost are unknown. [Codex rate card](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001415-chatgpt-rate-card-enterprise-token-based-pricing) · [AI Agents That Matter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.01502)

Freeze the six known tasks in tuning and reserve new repositories for confirmation before the first release. Report paired readiness counts, cost and wall ratios, complete-pair counts, repository-level uncertainty and the joint outcome. An equal-or-better quality result at lower cost and wall time is a simultaneous win. A single sample per repository measures this panel. Confirmation scores disclosed at baseline can serve as a regression suite; a subsequent promotion claim needs unexposed tasks or a prospective locked protocol.
